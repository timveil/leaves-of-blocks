A fairer deal when your board gets messy

• The game now reads how much room is actually left before dealing your next blocks, so letting the grid get cluttered has real consequences again
• After a new personal best, Leaves of Blocks may ask for a rating — at most once per version, never mid-game, and only once you've played a few rounds
• The Continue Game score on the home screen now shows a thousands separator, matching every other score in the game
• Shortened scores like 12.5K now follow your region's number format, so players in Germany or France see 12,5K like everywhere else

Thanks so much for playing Leaves of Blocks!
