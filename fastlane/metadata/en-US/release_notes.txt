Version 2.0.5 is a behind-the-scenes polish update focused on making the game feel smoother and more reliable in all the small ways that matter.

One change you may notice right away: your music will now keep playing while you enjoy Leaves of Blocks. The game no longer interrupts audio from other apps, so you can put on your favorite playlist or podcast and drop blocks to your heart's content without missing a beat.

If you use Game Center, things should feel noticeably more solid. Leaderboard scores and achievements now retry automatically if a submission doesn't go through the first time, so your progress is always recorded even if your connection has a hiccup. We also made several improvements under the hood to keep Game Center integration running smoothly and reliably.

We squashed a handful of bugs as well. Dragging blocks near the edge of the board should now behave exactly as you would expect, and we fixed a subtle issue where removing a placed piece could affect the wrong block on the board. Game history stats will now calculate correctly in all sessions, and a rare edge case in puzzle generation has been resolved to ensure every puzzle always has a valid solution. We also improved how VoiceOver interacts with decorative elements, so the experience is cleaner for anyone who relies on accessibility features.

Thank you for playing Leaves of Blocks!
