Hello puzzle lovers! This release brings optional Apple Game Center support to Leaves of Blocks.

Game Center is opt-in and off by default — head to Settings to enable it whenever you'd like to compete on the leaderboard or earn achievements for your best plays. Leave it off and the game stays exactly as it has always been: yours, on your device.

The privacy promise hasn't changed: no ads, no third-party tracking, no data sold. Game Center submissions go directly to Apple and only when you choose to opt in.

Thank you for playing Leaves of Blocks!
