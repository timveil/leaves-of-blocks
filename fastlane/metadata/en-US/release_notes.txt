Version 2.0.6 brings a few friendly new ways to keep your run going when the board gets tricky.

Changed your mind about a placement? You can now Undo your last block, once per game, to take it back and try a different spot. Feeling stuck? Tap Hint, also once per game, and we'll suggest a move to get you going again. And when a game finally comes to an end, you can now View Board to take one last look at your final grid and see how it all came together.

We also smoothed out a few rough edges. Dropping blocks near the top of the board now feels exactly as you'd expect, and we fixed a first-launch hiccup where the grid didn't always appear right away.

Thank you for playing Leaves of Blocks!
